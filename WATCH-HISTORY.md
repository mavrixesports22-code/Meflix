# Watch history and Continue Watching

The homepage has one **Continue Watching** section, populated only by actual unfinished playback. Separate Recently Played and Now Playing homepage rows, placeholder history cards and the extra hero pager have been removed. Finished titles remain accessible through **Watch history** in the browse/profile menu. Public catalogue rows named Continue Watching, Recently Played/Watched or Now Playing are excluded so they cannot duplicate the local section.

History stores up to 100 title/episode records in this browser's `meflix_watch_history_v1` local storage. It saves metadata, episode/season identifiers, position, duration and last-played time. Playback URLs, authentication tokens and credentials are excluded. Positions are saved during playback and immediately on pause, minimize and close. Failed startup attempts do not create history. A title's latest episode determines its Continue Watching state; completed episodes are excluded. Resume restores the exact recorded season, episode and position.

History entries can be removed individually or cleared from the history page. History is local to this browser/device and is not synced to another account or device. Legacy per-episode position bookmarks are still honored by the player. Those older bookmarks did not contain title metadata, so they cannot reconstruct a complete historical list of titles watched before this feature existed.

The player offers **Browse while watching** as an optional mini player, with expand, play/pause and close actions. This retains the same live media session instead of starting another stream. The homepage has no additional Now Playing banner.

On phones, Play/Pause and ten-second seek actions are grouped centrally. The compact play and seek targets follow the supplied Android APK's observed 64 and 48dp size variants; wider landscape layouts use the observed 84 variant. CSS pixels are used by the browser reconstruction. Exact Android rendering/experiment parity is not established. A labelled **Audio & Subtitles** sheet includes available audio languages and subtitles **Off**; **Episodes** opens the season/episode picker. The seek touch area is 44px high, sheets scroll when necessary, and close controls remain visible. Desktop controls retain their normal layout.

Focused REA evidence is retained in the original workspace at `outputs/netflix-mobile-apk/player-observations.md`. `node tools/check-watch-history.cjs` verifies persistence, exact episode identifiers, completion, removal, storage bounds, malformed data and exclusion of private fields. `node tools/check-player-reliability.cjs` also exercises pause/close saves, resume and mini-player lifecycle. Live browser checks cover one homepage section, exact-position resume and phone player controls in portrait and landscape.
